## Interpretation and evidence discipline

Four affine thresholds in three real dimensions admit at most15 strict patterns; the accepted margin excludes ties. Historical14/16 is not an optimality proof. Removing receiver biases gives central-plane bound14, not the primary affine bound. Constructive fixtures establish representational capacity and are never labeled learned. Random outcomes separately distinguish optimization and finite heldout combination recovery. Alternative splits and hidden custody are owned by the existing NL6 judge, not producer. The tiny domain does not support a population generalization claim. All seeds, failed checks and alternatives remain visible. Actual byte/header/schema/checkpoint/code/storage costs are separate from quantization spacing and timing; no throughput or economics superiority is inferred.
